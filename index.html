Byron Gmail

A private, read-only email tool for Byron Bunning's own Gmail accounts.

What it does

Byron Gmail is a small program that runs only on Byron Bunning's personal computer. It lets his AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic) search and read email across several of his own Gmail accounts from one place, so he does not have to switch between inboxes.

  • It can search for emails and read emails and threads.
  • It cannot send, delete, label, archive or change any email. It requests read-only access only.
  • It is used only by Byron Bunning, with his own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

How your data is handled

Sign-in tokens are stored in the computer's secure keychain, and email is fetched directly from Google to that computer when a search or read is requested. Full details are in the privacy policy.
